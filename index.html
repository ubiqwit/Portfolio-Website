mortgage-manager / dashboard

  • Portfolio dashboard

    Capital deployed, interest run-rate, weighted rate and CLTV, arrears, a maturity ladder and concentration by position, property type and city.

  • Bank reconciliation

    Imports CSV, Excel and OFX exports from any Canadian bank and matches deposits to mortgages by learned description, borrower, amount and due date.

  • Real mortgage maths

    Fixed or prime-plus-spread with floors, Canadian semi-annual compounding, interest-only or blended payments, daily accrual, and renewals that keep historical terms.

  • Month-end close

    A per-loan income report exported to Excel with live formulas — then the month is locked so the numbers sent to the accountant can’t drift.

  • Live market data

    Bank of Canada policy rate, prime, GoC yields and CPI, plus what a ±25 or 50 bp prime move does to the book’s income.

  • Built for real money

    Admin, editor and read-only roles, account lockout, CSRF and CSP, an audit log of every change, and tests on SQLite and PostgreSQL in CI.
